## Design decisions requiring care

1. Current move plan puts aggregate AppProperties under settings.configuration. Design requires splitting owner/support-specific surfaces and forbids merely relocating aggregate configuration while preserving all foreign imports. The relocation is an intermediate step, not final compliance.
2. `SchemaBootstrapService` construction-time side effect must not silently become later ApplicationRunner work; likewise seedDefaultProfile must not gain outer transaction merely because startup wiring moves.
3. Narrow public AI execution/model surfaces cannot expose secret-bearing AiProfileNode or SDK ChatModel in fact contracts. Changing access must preserve captured-model reuse even after a profile revision update.
4. All historical golden fixture gaps above must be recorded as gaps, not marked as verified from the 714-test baseline. A new test generated from new implementation alone does not establish historical compatibility; freeze source baseline expected values first.
